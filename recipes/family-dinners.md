# Family dinners

One pot for everyone, with a plain portion taken out for the baby before salt and spice go in. Quantities serve 2 adults, the 4-year-old and a baby portion.

Every recipe has a **Baby portion** step. The 4-year-old's portion is taken out before the chilli.

## Indian

### Sambar rice (bisi bele style, mild)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Serves:** 3
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 40 min

**Ingredients**

- 1 cup rice
- ½ cup toor dal
- 1 tbsp tamarind
- 1 tbsp sambar powder
- 1 carrot
- 6 beans
- 1 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp salt

**Steps**

1. Pressure cook rice and dal together with vegetables.
2. Add tamarind water, sambar powder and salt and simmer.
3. Before adding salt and sambar powder, set aside plain rice, dal and vegetables for the baby and mash them.

**Notes:** Not checked for the baby as written: take the baby's portion out before salt and spice.

### Dal tadka with jeera rice

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
- **Time:** 35 min

**Ingredients**

- ¾ cup toor dal
- 1 tomato, chopped
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 tsp ginger garlic paste
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp jeera
- 1 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp kashmiri chilli powder
- 1½ cups rice
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp coriander leaves, chopped

**Steps**

1. Pressure cook dal with turmeric and 2½ cups water for 4 whistles. Cook the rice with ½ tsp jeera.
2. Baby portion: take out 3 tbsp of plain dal and 2 tbsp of rice before any salt, mash together with a drop of ghee.
3. Heat ghee, add the rest of the jeera, then onion and ginger garlic paste and cook until golden. Add tomato and salt and cook down.
4. Kid portion: take out a ladle of dal and mix in a spoon of the tempering now.
5. Add chilli powder to the rest of the tempering, pour over the dal and simmer 5 minutes. Finish with coriander.

### Palak paneer with roti

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
- **Time:** 35 min
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 1 bunch spinach
- 200 g paneer, cubed
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 tomato, chopped
- 1 tsp ginger garlic paste
- 1 tsp jeera
- ½ tsp garam masala
- 2 tbsp cream
- 1 tbsp ghee
- 1 green chilli
- 1 tsp salt
- 6 roti

**Steps**

1. Blanch the spinach for 2 minutes, cool in cold water and blend smooth.
2. Baby portion: mash 2 tbsp of the plain spinach puree with a few crumbled cubes of paneer and a little warm water. No salt.
3. Heat ghee with jeera, cook onion, ginger garlic paste and tomato until soft, then add spinach puree, garam masala and salt.
4. Kid portion: take out a small bowl now, add paneer and a spoon of cream.
5. Add the green chilli, the rest of the paneer and cream, and simmer 5 minutes. Serve with roti.

### Chana masala with jeera rice (mild)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Punjabi
- **Time:** 45 min

**Ingredients**

- 1 cup chickpeas, soaked overnight
- 2 tomatoes, pureed
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 tsp ginger garlic paste
- 1 tbsp chana masala powder
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice
- 1 tsp jeera

**Steps**

1. Pressure cook the chickpeas with turmeric for 6 whistles until very soft. Cook rice with jeera.
2. Baby portion: blend 3 tbsp of plain chickpeas with their cooking water until smooth. No salt.
3. Cook onion and ginger garlic paste in oil until golden, add tomato puree and salt and cook until oil separates.
4. Add chickpeas with some cooking water, mash a few, and simmer 10 minutes. Kid portion: take out a bowl before the masala powder.
5. Stir in chana masala powder and simmer 5 more minutes.

### Mild vegetable biryani with raita

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Hyderabadi
- **Time:** 50 min
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 1½ cups basmati rice, soaked 20 minutes
- 2 cups mixed vegetables, carrot, beans, peas and potato
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- ½ cup curd, plus 1 cup for raita
- 1 tsp ginger garlic paste
- 1 tbsp biryani masala
- 2 tbsp ghee
- 1 tbsp mint leaves
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ cucumber, grated

**Steps**

1. Baby portion: steam a handful of the carrot, potato and peas until very soft and mash them with a spoon of plain cooked rice before anything is seasoned.
2. Fry the onion in ghee until golden. Add ginger garlic paste, vegetables, ½ cup curd, biryani masala and salt, and cook 5 minutes.
3. Boil the rice until 70% cooked and drain.
4. Layer rice over the vegetables, sprinkle mint, cover tightly and cook on the lowest heat for 20 minutes.
5. Mix 1 cup curd with grated cucumber and a pinch of salt for raita.

### Mutta kuzhambu (South Indian egg curry)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Tamil
- **Time:** 35 min
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg

**Ingredients**

- 5 eggs, hard boiled and peeled
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 tbsp tamarind, pulp
- 1 tbsp kuzhambu powder
- ½ tsp turmeric
- ½ cup coconut milk
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 8 curry leaves
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Baby portion: mash one fully cooked egg with 2 tbsp of plain cooked rice and a drop of ghee.
2. Temper mustard seeds and curry leaves in oil, add onion and cook until soft, then tomato, turmeric and salt until mushy.
3. Add tamarind water and kuzhambu powder and simmer 10 minutes, then coconut milk.
4. Slit the eggs and simmer them in the gravy for 5 minutes. Serve with rice.

**Notes:** For the kid, serve an egg with a spoon of gravy mixed with plenty of rice.

### Pav bhaji

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Mumbai
- **Time:** 40 min

**Ingredients**

- 3 potatoes
- 1 cup cauliflower
- ½ cup green peas
- 1 carrot
- 1 bell pepper, finely chopped
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp pav bhaji masala
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1 tsp salt
- 8 pav buns
- 1 lemon

**Steps**

1. Pressure cook potatoes, cauliflower, peas and carrot until very soft.
2. Baby portion: take out 3 tbsp of the plain vegetables and mash with a little butter. No salt.
3. Cook onion and bell pepper in butter, add tomatoes and salt, then the mashed vegetables, and mash everything together.
4. Kid portion: take out a bowl now, with an extra dab of butter.
5. Add pav bhaji masala and simmer 10 minutes. Toast the pav in butter and serve with lemon.

### Mild butter chicken with naan

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Punjabi
- **Time:** 45 min
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 400 g chicken, boneless thigh, cubed
- ½ cup curd
- 1 tsp ginger garlic paste
- 1 cup tomato puree
- 3 tbsp butter
- ¼ cup cream
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp kasuri methi
- 1 tsp kashmiri chilli powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 4 naan

**Steps**

1. Marinate chicken in curd, ginger garlic paste and half the salt for 30 minutes.
2. Sear the chicken in 1 tbsp butter until cooked through.
3. Baby portion: take out a few pieces of plain cooked chicken and blend or mince very finely with a spoon of tomato puree and water. No salt in the baby's part.
4. Cook tomato puree in the rest of the butter with garam masala, chilli powder and salt for 10 minutes. Stir in cream, kasuri methi and the chicken.

## Mexican American

### Chicken tinga tacos (mild)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Mexican American
- **Time:** 40 min
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 400 g chicken breast
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 chipotle pepper in adobo, for the adults
- 12 small corn tortillas
- ½ cup queso fresco, crumbled
- 1 avocado
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp oil

**Steps**

1. Simmer the chicken in water until cooked, then shred it.
2. Baby portion: mince a little plain shredded chicken very finely and mash with avocado and warm water.
3. Blend tomatoes, garlic and oregano. Cook the onion in oil, add the sauce and salt, and simmer 5 minutes, then add chicken.
4. Kid portion: take out a bowl before adding chipotle. Stir chipotle into the rest.
5. Serve in warm tortillas with queso fresco and avocado.

### Cilantro lime rice burrito bowls

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Mexican American
- **Time:** 35 min
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 1½ cups rice
- 1 lemon, juiced
- ¼ cup coriander leaves, chopped
- 1½ cups black beans, cooked
- 1 cup corn
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 cup cheddar cheese, grated
- ½ cup sour cream
- 1 avocado
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp oil

**Steps**

1. Cook the rice. Baby portion: mash 2 tbsp plain rice with 2 tbsp unsalted black beans and some avocado.
2. Stir lemon juice, coriander and half the salt into the rest of the rice.
3. Saute pepper and onion in oil with cumin and salt. Warm the beans and corn.
4. Build bowls: rice, beans, corn, peppers, cheese, sour cream and avocado.

### Sopa de fideo (tomato noodle soup)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Mexican
- **Time:** 25 min
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 1 cup vermicelli, fideo
- 3 tomatoes
- ½ onion
- 1 garlic clove
- 4 cups vegetable stock, low sodium
- 1 carrot, diced small
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ cup queso fresco, crumbled
- 1 lemon

**Steps**

1. Blend tomatoes, onion and garlic smooth.
2. Toast the vermicelli in oil until golden. Add the tomato blend and cook 3 minutes.
3. Add stock and carrot and simmer 10 minutes until the noodles and carrot are soft.
4. Baby portion: before adding salt, take out a few spoons of noodles and carrot and mash them; use water instead of stock for the baby's portion if your stock has salt.
5. Season with salt. Serve with queso fresco and a squeeze of lemon.

### Mild black bean and cheese enchiladas

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Tex-Mex
- **Time:** 45 min
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 8 corn tortillas
- 2 cups black beans, cooked
- 1 cup sweet potato, cubed and roasted
- 1½ cups Monterey Jack cheese, grated
- 1½ cups mild enchilada sauce
- ½ onion, finely chopped
- 1 tsp cumin
- ½ tsp salt

**Steps**

1. Baby portion: mash 2 tbsp of unsalted beans with 2 tbsp roasted sweet potato.
2. Mix the rest of the beans with sweet potato, onion, cumin, salt and half the cheese.
3. Fill and roll the tortillas, place in a baking dish, cover with sauce and the rest of the cheese.
4. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 20 minutes until bubbling.

## Around the world

### Teriyaki tofu and vegetable rice bowl

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Japanese American
- **Time:** 30 min

**Ingredients**

- 400 g firm tofu, cubed
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 1 carrot, sliced
- 3 tbsp soy sauce, low sodium
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp ginger, grated
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1½ cups rice
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

**Steps**

1. Cook rice. Steam broccoli and carrot until tender.
2. Baby portion: mash some plain steamed broccoli and carrot with a little plain tofu. No sauce for the baby (it has salt and honey).
3. Pan-fry tofu in sesame oil until golden.
4. Whisk soy sauce, honey, ginger, cornstarch and 3 tbsp water, pour over tofu and cook until glossy. Toss in vegetables and top with sesame seeds.

### Pasta primavera with parmesan

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Italian American
- **Time:** 25 min
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 250 g penne pasta
- 1 zucchini, diced
- 1 cup broccoli florets
- ½ cup green peas
- 1 cup tomato, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, sliced
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- ½ cup parmesan cheese, grated
- ½ cup milk
- 1 tsp salt

**Steps**

1. Boil pasta, adding broccoli and peas for the last 3 minutes. Drain, keeping a cup of the water.
2. Baby portion: take out a few spoons of plain pasta and vegetables before salting and blend or mash with a little milk.
3. Cook garlic and zucchini in olive oil, add tomato and cook 5 minutes.
4. Toss in pasta, vegetables, milk, parmesan and salt, loosening with pasta water.

### Lebanese lentil soup with pita

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Lebanese
- **Time:** 35 min

**Ingredients**

- 1 cup masoor dal
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 carrot, chopped
- 1 potato, chopped
- 1 tsp cumin
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon
- 1 tsp salt
- 4 pita bread

**Steps**

1. Soften onion in olive oil, then add lentils, carrot, potato, cumin and 5 cups water.
2. Simmer 25 minutes until everything is very soft, then blend smooth.
3. Baby portion: take out a small bowl before salt and lemon.
4. Season with salt and lemon juice. Serve with warm pita.
