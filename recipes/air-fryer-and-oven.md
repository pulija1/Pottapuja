# Air fryer and oven recipes

Recipes made for a two-basket air fryer (like the Ninja dual zone) and a conventional oven: less standing at the stove, and two parts of the meal cooking at once. Each one also says how to make it in the oven or the other appliance, so it works with whichever the family has.

With two baskets, put the longer item in basket 1 and the shorter in basket 2 and press **Sync** (Ninja also calls it Smart Finish): basket 2 starts later and both finish together. With one basket, cook them one after the other.

Air fryer times are for a preheated basket and food spread in one layer. Ovens and air fryers vary, so check the first time. Chicken and eggs are always cooked through.

Many recipes in the other files also have an **Air fryer** or **Oven** line, for example the vepudu (fries) in [telugu-curries.md](telugu-curries.md) and the egg muffins and paneer tikka bites in [kid-4-years.md](kid-4-years.md).

## Family dinners

### Paneer tikka with masala potato wedges

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
- **Time:** 35 min
- **Air fryer:** 20 min at 200°C. Both baskets at once: potato wedges in basket 1 for 20 minutes, paneer tikka in basket 2 for 10 minutes, with Sync so they finish together. Shake the wedges halfway.
- **Oven:** 30 min at 220°C. Wedges on a lined tray for 30 minutes; add the paneer tikka on a second tray for the last 12 minutes.

**Ingredients**

- 200 g paneer, cut in big cubes
- ½ cup curd, thick
- 1 tsp tandoori masala, mild
- 1 capsicum, cut in squares
- 1 onion, cut in petals
- 3 potato, cut in wedges
- 2 tbsp oil
- ½ tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp chaat masala
- 1 tsp salt
- 6 roti

**Steps**

1. Baby portion: take out 2 tbsp of plain paneer and steam or warm it, then crumble it soft with a little potato from step 3 before any salt or masala.
2. Mix curd, tandoori masala, half the salt and 1 tsp oil. Coat the paneer, capsicum and onion and rest while the wedges start.
3. Toss the potato wedges with the rest of the oil, turmeric and the rest of the salt. Air fry at 200°C for 20 minutes in basket 1, shaking halfway.
4. Put the paneer and vegetables in basket 2 at 200°C for 10 minutes and press Sync, so both baskets finish together.
5. Take the kid's portion out, then sprinkle chaat masala on the rest. Serve with roti and curd or mint chutney.

**Notes:** In the oven, use 220°C: wedges 30 minutes, paneer on its own tray for the last 12.

### Tandoori chicken drumsticks with roasted vegetables

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
- **Time:** 40 min
- **Air fryer:** 22 min at 200°C. Both baskets at once: drumsticks in basket 1 for 22 minutes, turned at 11, and the vegetables in basket 2 for 15 minutes, with Sync. The chicken is done when the juices run clear and there is no pink at the bone.
- **Oven:** 35 min at 220°C. Drumsticks and vegetables on one lined tray, turned at 20 minutes.

**Ingredients**

- 6 chicken drumsticks, skin off and slashed
- ½ cup curd, thick
- 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
- 2 tsp tandoori masala, mild
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 carrot, cut in thick rounds
- 1 cup cauliflower, florets
- 1 capsicum, cut in squares
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Baby portion: coat one drumstick in plain curd and turmeric only, no salt or masala, and cook it with the rest. Shred the meat very finely and mash it with soft rice and a little water.
2. Mix curd, ginger garlic paste, tandoori masala, lemon juice and salt, and coat the rest of the drumsticks. Rest 10 minutes while the rice cooks.
3. Air fry the drumsticks at 200°C in basket 1 for 22 minutes, turning at 11.
4. Toss the vegetables with oil and a pinch of salt, put them in basket 2 at 200°C for 15 minutes, and press Sync so both finish together.
5. Check the chicken is cooked through at the bone. Shred the 4-year-old's chicken off the bone and serve with rice and vegetables.

**Notes:** Make it a day ahead: marinate overnight in the fridge and it cooks the same.

### Roasted gobi and aloo (cauliflower and potato) with roti

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
- **Time:** 30 min
- **Air fryer:** 18 min at 200°C. Spread the cauliflower in one basket and the potato in the other, shake at 9 minutes.
- **Oven:** 30 min at 220°C on a lined tray, turned at 15 minutes.

**Ingredients**

- 1 cauliflower, florets
- 2 potato, cut in small cubes
- 2 tbsp oil
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp jeera
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp coriander leaves, chopped
- 6 roti

**Steps**

1. Toss the cauliflower and potato with oil, turmeric and jeera.
2. Roast in the oven at 220°C for 30 minutes, turning at 15, until tender and browned at the edges.
3. Baby portion: take out a few very soft potato and cauliflower pieces before any salt or masala and mash them with ghee and warm water.
4. Take out the kid's portion, then toss the rest with coriander powder, garam masala and salt. Scatter coriander leaves and serve with roti and dal.

**Notes:** Add a handful of peas or paneer cubes for the last 5 minutes.

## The 4-year-old

### Air fryer egg and cheese toast cups

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Cuisine:** American
- **Time:** 12 min
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg · 🥛 Milk/dairy
- **Air fryer:** 10 min at 160°C in silicone muffin cups, until the white and yolk are fully set.
- **Oven:** 15 min at 180°C in a muffin tin.

**Ingredients**

- 2 slice whole wheat bread, crusts off
- 2 eggs
- 2 tbsp cheddar cheese, grated
- 1 tsp butter
- 1 pinch salt

**Steps**

1. Butter the bread, flatten each slice with a rolling pin and press it into a silicone muffin cup.
2. Crack an egg into each cup, add a pinch of salt and the cheese.
3. Air fry at 160°C for 10 minutes until the egg is fully set, with no runny yolk.

**Notes:** Add chopped spinach or capsicum under the egg. Cool 2 minutes before eating.

### Air fryer aloo tikki with peas

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** lunchbox
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
- **Time:** 25 min
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy
- **Air fryer:** 12 min at 200°C, brushed with oil and turned at 6 minutes, until crisp outside.
- **Oven:** 20 min at 220°C on a lined tray, turned once.

**Ingredients**

- 2 potato, boiled, peeled and mashed
- ¼ cup peas, cooked and mashed
- 2 tbsp paneer, grated
- 2 tbsp bread crumbs
- ¼ tsp jeera powder
- 1 tbsp coriander leaves, chopped
- 1 tsp oil
- 1 pinch salt

**Steps**

1. Mix the potato, mashed peas, paneer, bread crumbs, jeera powder, coriander and salt.
2. Shape into 6 small flat tikkis and brush both sides with oil.
3. Air fry at 200°C for 12 minutes, turning at 6, until golden.
4. Cool before packing, with ketchup or curd in a small box.

**Notes:** Make a double batch and freeze half before cooking; air fry from frozen for 15 minutes.

## The 8-month-old

### Roasted sweet potato and carrot fingers

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** lunch
- **Cuisine:** American
- **Time:** 20 min
- **Texture:** soft finger food
- **Air fryer:** 18 min: steam 8 minutes, then 10 minutes at 170°C, shaken once, until soft right through.
- **Oven:** 30 min at 200°C in a covered dish, until very soft.

**Ingredients**

- 1 sweet potato, peeled, cut in fingers
- 1 carrot, peeled, cut in thick fingers
- ½ tsp ghee

**Steps**

1. Cut the sweet potato and carrot into fingers about as thick as an adult finger.
2. Steam them for 8 minutes first, then toss with ghee (no salt).
3. Air fry at 170°C for 10 minutes until soft right through.
4. Check every piece squashes easily between your finger and thumb before giving it. Cool fully.

**Notes:** Soft finger food: no crisp edges. Peel off anything hard. Always sit with the baby while they eat.
