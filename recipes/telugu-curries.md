# Telugu curries and pappu

Andhra and Telangana home cooking for the whole family: pappu, kura, vepudu, pulusu and charu, eaten with rice. Each recipe uses the Telugu name with the English in brackets, and the vegetables are written the way the kitchen list knows them (`brinjal (vankaya)`), so typing either name in the app finds them.

Every recipe has a **Baby portion** step: take a plain portion out before salt, chilli and tamarind go in, and mash it. The 4-year-old's portion is taken out before the chilli, or the chilli is left out and added to the adults' plates.

## Pappu (dal)

### Tomato pappu (tomato dal)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 30 min
- **Family favorite:** yes

**Ingredients**

- ¾ cup toor dal (kandi pappu)
- 3 tomato (tamata), chopped
- 1 onion (ullipaya), sliced
- 2 green chilli (pachi mirapakaya), slit
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp jeera
- 2 clove garlic (vellulli), crushed
- 8 curry leaves
- 1 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Pressure cook the dal with turmeric and 2 cups water for 3 to 4 whistles, until very soft.
2. Baby portion: take out 3 tbsp of the plain cooked dal, mash it smooth with a little cooked rice and a drop of ghee.
3. Cook the onion and tomato in a pan until mushy, add the green chillies and salt, then the mashed dal and simmer 5 minutes.
4. Heat ghee, splutter mustard seeds and jeera, add garlic and curry leaves, and pour over the pappu. Serve with rice.

**Notes:** For the kid, take a portion out before the green chillies go in.

### Palakura pappu (spinach dal)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 30 min

**Ingredients**

- ¾ cup toor dal (kandi pappu)
- 1 bunch spinach (palakura), chopped
- 1 tomato (tamata), chopped
- 1 onion (ullipaya), chopped
- 2 green chilli (pachi mirapakaya), slit
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp jeera
- 2 clove garlic (vellulli), crushed
- 1 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Pressure cook the dal, spinach, tomato, onion and turmeric together for 3 whistles.
2. Baby portion: take out 3 tbsp before salt and chilli and mash or blend it smooth.
3. Add salt and green chillies to the rest and mash everything with a pappu gutti or the back of a ladle.
4. Temper mustard seeds, jeera and garlic in ghee and pour over. Serve with rice and a little ghee.

**Notes:** Spinach dal is a gentle way to get greens into the 4-year-old; mix with plenty of rice and ghee.

### Mamidikaya pappu (raw mango dal)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 30 min

**Ingredients**

- ¾ cup toor dal (kandi pappu)
- 1 raw mango (mamidikaya), peeled and cubed
- 1 onion (ullipaya), sliced
- 2 green chilli (pachi mirapakaya), slit
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp jeera
- 8 curry leaves
- 1 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Baby portion: pressure cook the dal with turmeric and take out 3 tbsp of plain dal before the mango goes in; mash it with rice.
2. Cook the mango, onion and green chillies in a little water until the mango is soft.
3. Add the cooked dal and salt and simmer 5 minutes.
4. Temper mustard seeds, jeera and curry leaves in ghee and pour over. Serve with rice.

**Notes:** Raw mango is sour; for the kid, use less mango in their portion or mix with extra rice and ghee.

## Kura and vepudu (curries and fries)

### Vankaya kura (brinjal curry)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 30 min

**Ingredients**

- 6 brinjal (vankaya), small, cut in pieces
- 1 onion (ullipaya), chopped
- 2 tomato (tamata), chopped
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 8 curry leaves
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Heat oil, splutter mustard seeds and curry leaves, and cook the onion until soft.
2. Add the brinjal, tomato and turmeric, cover and cook on low for 12 minutes until soft.
3. Baby portion: take out a few soft pieces of brinjal before salt and chilli and mash them with rice and a drop of ghee.
4. Take the kid's portion out, then add salt, chilli powder and coriander powder to the rest and cook 5 more minutes. Serve with rice or roti.

**Notes:** Soak cut brinjal in water so it doesn't turn dark.

### Gutti vankaya kura (stuffed brinjal curry)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 45 min
- **Good for:** 🥜 Nuts

**Ingredients**

- 8 brinjal (vankaya), small, slit in a cross
- 3 tbsp peanut powder
- 2 tbsp sesame seeds, roasted and ground
- 2 tbsp grated coconut
- 1 onion (ullipaya), chopped
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp tamarind, pulp
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 3 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Mix the peanut powder, sesame, coconut, coriander powder and turmeric into a stuffing.
2. Baby portion: steam 2 brinjals plain until very soft, scoop out the flesh and mash it with rice.
3. Take a little stuffing out for the kid, then add chilli powder, tamarind and salt to the rest and fill the brinjals.
4. Cook the onion in oil, add the brinjals and any leftover stuffing with ½ cup water, cover and cook on low 20 minutes, turning gently.

**Notes:** The nuts are ground, so it's fine for the 4-year-old. Cook the kid's brinjals with the mild stuffing.

### Bendakaya vepudu (okra fry)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 25 min

**Ingredients**

- 250 g okra (bendakaya), dried well and cut in rounds
- 1 onion (ullipaya), chopped
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp jeera
- 8 curry leaves
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice
- ¾ cup toor dal (kandi pappu)

**Steps**

1. Heat oil, splutter mustard seeds, jeera and curry leaves, add onion and okra and fry on medium, uncovered, until the okra is no longer sticky (about 15 minutes).
2. Baby portion: steam a handful of okra separately until very soft and mash it into plain cooked dal and rice.
3. Take the kid's portion out, then add turmeric, chilli powder and salt to the rest and fry 3 more minutes.
4. Serve with rice and pappu.

**Notes:** Don't cover the pan or the okra turns slimy.

### Bangaladumpa vepudu (potato fry)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 25 min
- **Family favorite:** yes

**Ingredients**

- 4 potato (bangaladumpa), boiled, peeled and cubed
- 1 onion (ullipaya), sliced
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp chana dal
- 1 tsp urad dal
- 8 curry leaves
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Baby portion: mash 2 tbsp of plain boiled potato with a little warm milk or ghee.
2. Heat oil, splutter mustard seeds, chana dal, urad dal and curry leaves, then cook the onion until golden.
3. Add the potatoes and turmeric and fry until crisp at the edges. Take the kid's portion out.
4. Add chilli powder and salt to the rest and toss well. Serve with rice and pappu or charu.

**Notes:** Kids usually love this one with pappu annam (dal rice).

### Dondakaya vepudu (ivy gourd fry)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 30 min

**Ingredients**

- 250 g ivy gourd (dondakaya), cut in thin rounds
- 1 onion (ullipaya), chopped
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 2 tbsp grated coconut
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 8 curry leaves
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice
- ¾ cup toor dal (kandi pappu)

**Steps**

1. Heat oil, splutter mustard seeds and curry leaves, add the ivy gourd and onion, and fry on low, partly covered, until soft and browned (about 20 minutes).
2. Baby portion: serve the baby plain cooked dal and rice mashed together, since ivy gourd stays firm.
3. Take the kid's portion out and chop it finely, then add turmeric, chilli powder, salt and coconut to the rest.
4. Serve with rice and pappu.

**Notes:** Cut the rounds thin so they cook through and soften.

### Aratikaya vepudu (raw banana fry)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 25 min

**Ingredients**

- 2 raw banana (aratikaya), peeled and cubed
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp urad dal
- 8 curry leaves
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Boil the raw banana cubes with turmeric for 5 minutes until just soft and drain.
2. Baby portion: take out a few cubes, boil them a little longer and mash smooth with ghee.
3. Heat oil, splutter mustard seeds, urad dal and curry leaves, add the banana and fry until golden. Take the kid's portion out.
4. Add chilli powder and salt to the rest and toss. Serve with rice and charu.

**Notes:** Rub a little oil on your hands before peeling raw banana; the sap is sticky.

### Sorakaya senagapappu kura (bottle gourd and chana dal curry)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 30 min

**Ingredients**

- ½ bottle gourd (sorakaya), peeled and cubed
- ¼ cup chana dal, soaked 1 hour
- 1 onion (ullipaya), chopped
- 1 tomato (tamata), chopped
- 2 green chilli (pachi mirapakaya), slit
- ½ tsp turmeric
- ¼ cup milk
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 8 curry leaves
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Cook the bottle gourd and soaked chana dal with turmeric and a little water until soft (2 whistles).
2. Baby portion: take out a few soft pieces of bottle gourd before salt and mash them smooth.
3. Temper mustard seeds and curry leaves in oil, cook the onion and tomato, then add the bottle gourd and dal, green chillies and salt.
4. Stir in the milk and simmer 3 minutes. Serve with rice or roti.

**Notes:** Sorakaya is mild and soft, so it's an easy vegetable for both children.

### Beerakaya kura (ridge gourd curry)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 25 min

**Ingredients**

- 2 ridge gourd (beerakaya), peeled and cubed
- 1 onion (ullipaya), chopped
- 1 tomato (tamata), chopped
- 2 green chilli (pachi mirapakaya), slit
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp jeera
- 8 curry leaves
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Heat oil, splutter mustard seeds, jeera and curry leaves, and cook the onion until soft.
2. Add the ridge gourd, tomato and turmeric, cover and cook 10 minutes; it releases its own water.
3. Baby portion: take out a few soft pieces before salt and chilli and mash them with rice.
4. Take the kid's portion out, then add green chillies and salt to the rest and cook 3 more minutes. Serve with rice.

**Notes:** A spoon of milk or a little peanut powder at the end makes it creamier.

### Cabbage pesarapappu kura (cabbage and moong dal fry)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 25 min

**Ingredients**

- ½ cabbage, finely shredded
- ¼ cup moong dal (pesara pappu), soaked 30 minutes
- 2 green chilli (pachi mirapakaya), slit
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 2 tbsp grated coconut
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp jeera
- 8 curry leaves
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Heat oil, splutter mustard seeds, jeera and curry leaves, then add the soaked moong dal and cabbage with turmeric.
2. Cover and cook on low for 12 minutes until the dal is soft.
3. Baby portion: take out 2 tbsp before salt and chilli and mash it with a little rice and ghee.
4. Take the kid's portion out, then add green chillies, salt and coconut to the rest. Serve with rice and pappu or charu.

## Pulusu and charu (tamarind stews and rasam)

### Munagakaya pulusu (drumstick tamarind stew)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 35 min

**Ingredients**

- 2 drumstick (munagakaya), cut in finger lengths
- 1 onion (ullipaya), sliced
- 1 tomato (tamata), chopped
- 2 tbsp tamarind, pulp
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp jaggery
- 1 tsp rice flour
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 8 curry leaves
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Baby portion: boil one drumstick piece in plain water until soft, scrape out the soft flesh and mash it into plain rice and dal.
2. Temper mustard seeds and curry leaves in oil, cook the onion and tomato, then add the drumsticks, turmeric and 2 cups water and cook until soft.
3. Add the tamarind water, chilli powder, coriander powder, salt and jaggery and simmer 10 minutes.
4. Stir in the rice flour mixed with a little water to thicken. Serve hot with rice.

**Notes:** For the kid, serve a spoon of pulusu mixed with lots of rice and ghee, and scrape the flesh off the drumstick for them.

### Mukkala pulusu (mixed vegetable pulusu)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 35 min

**Ingredients**

- 1 drumstick (munagakaya), cut in pieces
- 1 cup pumpkin (gummadikaya), cubed
- 2 brinjal (vankaya), cut in pieces
- 1 onion (ullipaya), sliced
- 2 tbsp tamarind, pulp
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp jaggery
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp fenugreek seeds
- 8 curry leaves
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Cook the vegetables and onion with turmeric in 2 cups water until soft.
2. Baby portion: take out a few pieces of pumpkin before tamarind and salt and mash them smooth.
3. Add the tamarind water, chilli powder, salt and jaggery and simmer 10 minutes.
4. Temper mustard seeds, fenugreek seeds and curry leaves in oil and pour over. Serve with rice.

**Notes:** Pulusu tastes even better the next day.

### Majjiga pulusu (buttermilk curry)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 25 min
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 2 cups curd (perugu), whisked with 1 cup water
- 1 cup bottle gourd (sorakaya), cubed
- 1 tbsp chana dal, soaked
- 1 tbsp grated coconut
- 1 inch ginger (allam)
- 2 green chilli (pachi mirapakaya)
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 8 curry leaves
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Boil the bottle gourd in a little water until soft.
2. Baby portion: take out a few soft pieces and mash them with a spoon of curd and rice.
3. Grind the soaked chana dal, coconut, ginger and green chillies to a paste, stir it into the curd with turmeric and salt, add the bottle gourd and heat gently without boiling.
4. Temper mustard seeds and curry leaves in oil and pour over. Serve with rice.

**Notes:** For the kid, make the paste with one chilli and keep their portion aside before tempering, or use no chilli at all.

### Tomato charu (tomato rasam)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 20 min
- **Family favorite:** yes

**Ingredients**

- 3 tomato (tamata), chopped
- 1 tbsp tamarind, pulp
- 2 tbsp toor dal (kandi pappu), cooked
- 1 tsp rasam powder
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 2 clove garlic (vellulli), crushed
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp jeera
- 8 curry leaves
- 2 tbsp coriander leaves (kothimeera), chopped
- 1 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Baby portion: mash 2 tbsp of the plain cooked dal with rice and a drop of ghee.
2. Boil the tomatoes with turmeric in 2 cups water until soft and mash them.
3. Add the tamarind water, cooked dal, rasam powder and salt and simmer 5 minutes; don't let it boil hard.
4. Temper mustard seeds, jeera, garlic and curry leaves in ghee, pour over and add coriander. Serve with rice and a vepudu.

**Notes:** For the kid, a little charu mixed with rice and ghee is a classic comfort meal.

## Egg and chicken

### Kodiguddu pulusu (egg tamarind curry)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 30 min
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg

**Ingredients**

- 5 eggs, hard boiled and peeled
- 2 onion (ullipaya), sliced
- 1 tomato (tamata), chopped
- 1 tbsp tamarind, pulp
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 8 curry leaves
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Baby portion: mash one fully cooked egg yolk with plain rice and a drop of ghee.
2. Kid portion: keep one boiled egg aside to serve with a spoon of the gravy before the chilli goes in.
3. Cook the onion in oil until golden, add tomato and turmeric until mushy, then the chilli powder, coriander powder, tamarind water and salt with 1 cup water.
4. Slit the eggs, simmer them in the pulusu 10 minutes, and temper mustard seeds and curry leaves on top. Serve with rice.

### Kodi kura (Andhra chicken curry, mild)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 45 min

**Ingredients**

- 500 g chicken, curry cut
- 2 onion (ullipaya), finely chopped
- 2 tomato (tamata), chopped
- 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
- ½ cup curd (perugu)
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 2 tbsp coriander leaves (kothimeera)
- 3 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt
- 1½ cups rice

**Steps**

1. Marinate the chicken in curd, turmeric and ginger garlic paste for 30 minutes.
2. Cook the onion in oil until golden, add the tomatoes and cook until soft, then add the chicken and cook covered 20 minutes until fully cooked.
3. Baby portion: take out a small piece of fully cooked chicken before salt and spice and shred it very finely into mashed rice and dal.
4. Take the kid's portion out, then add chilli powder, coriander powder, garam masala and salt to the rest and simmer 5 minutes. Finish with coriander leaves.

**Notes:** For the kid, shred the chicken and mix it into rice with a spoon of the mild gravy.
