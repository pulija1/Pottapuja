# Recipes for the 8-month-old

Soft, mashed and pureed food for a baby around 8 months, from South Indian, North Indian, Mexican, American, Mediterranean and East Asian kitchens.

Rules every recipe here follows (the app checks them too and drops a recipe that breaks one):

- **No honey** before 12 months (infant botulism).
- **No added salt or sugar**, and no jaggery, pickles, papad or stock cubes.
- **No whole or chopped nuts.** Nuts only as a fine powder or a thin nut butter stirred into food. Early, regular peanut and egg are recommended by paediatricians to lower allergy risk, so a few recipes here use them. Check with your doctor first if there is eczema or a known allergy.
- **Eggs fully cooked**, yolk and white.
- **Soft textures:** smooth puree, mashed, porridge, or soft finger food that squashes between your fingers.
- Cow's milk is fine **cooked into food**, but not as the main drink before 12 months. Plain whole-milk curd (yogurt) and paneer are fine.
- Introduce **one new food at a time** and wait 3 days before the next.

## South Indian

### Ragi porridge (ragi kanji)

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 10 min
- **Texture:** porridge

**Ingredients**

- 2 tbsp ragi flour
- 1 cup water
- ½ banana

**Steps**

1. Whisk ragi flour into cold water so there are no lumps.
2. Cook on low heat, stirring, for 6 to 8 minutes until thick and glossy.
3. Cool to lukewarm and stir in mashed ripe banana for sweetness.

**Notes:** Iron and calcium rich. Introduce one new food at a time.

### Soft idli mashed with dal water

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Serves:** 2
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Texture:** mashed
- **ID:** lib-idli-dal-mash

**Ingredients**

- ½ cup idli/dosa batter
- 1 tbsp moong dal
- ¼ tsp ghee

**Steps**

1. Steam a plain idli from the batter (no salt added to the baby's portion).
2. Cook moong dal until very soft and keep the dal water.
3. Mash the idli with dal water and a drop of ghee to a smooth paste.

### Soft rava porridge

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 10 min
- **Texture:** porridge
- **ID:** lib-rava-kanji

**Ingredients**

- 2 tbsp rava
- ½ tsp ghee
- 1 cup water
- ½ apple

**Steps**

1. Roast rava in ghee for 2 minutes on low heat.
2. Add water slowly while stirring, cook until soft.
3. Steam and mash the apple, and stir it in for natural sweetness.

### Moong dal khichdi (soft)

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** lunch
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 25 min
- **Texture:** mashed
- **ID:** lib-moong-khichdi

**Ingredients**

- 2 tbsp rice
- 1 tbsp moong dal
- ¼ carrot
- ½ tsp ghee
- 1 pinch turmeric
- 1½ cups water

**Steps**

1. Wash rice and moong dal well.
2. Pressure cook with chopped carrot, a pinch of turmeric and water for 4 to 5 whistles.
3. Mash well, loosen with warm water, and stir in ghee. No salt.

### Mashed curd rice

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** lunch
- **Serves:** 2

- **Texture:** mashed

**Ingredients**

### Toor dal and vegetable soup

- **ID:** lib-dal-soup

**Ingredients**

### Ragi apple porridge with peanut powder

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 12 min
- **Texture:** porridge
- **Good for:** 🥜 Nuts

**Ingredients**

- 1 tbsp ragi flour
- ¾ cup water
- ¼ apple, peeled and grated
- ½ tsp peanut powder, finely ground

**Steps**

1. Whisk ragi flour into the water until smooth.
2. Add the grated apple and cook on low heat, stirring, for 7 minutes until thick and the apple is soft.
3. Mash well, cool to lukewarm, and stir in the finely ground peanut powder.

**Notes:** An easy way to keep peanut in the baby's diet a few times a week once it has been introduced.

### Lauki moong dal mash

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** lunch
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 20 min
- **Texture:** mashed

**Ingredients**

- 2 tbsp moong dal
- ¼ cup bottle gourd, peeled and cubed
- 1 pinch turmeric
- 1 pinch jeera
- ¼ tsp ghee
- 1 cup water

**Steps**

1. Wash the moong dal and pressure cook with bottle gourd, turmeric, jeera and water for 3 whistles.
2. Mash to a smooth paste and stir in ghee. No salt.

### Soft steamed idli fingers with ghee

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** snack
- **Serves:** 2
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 15 min
- **Texture:** soft finger food

**Ingredients**

- ½ cup idli/dosa batter
- ¼ tsp ghee

**Steps**

1. Steam a plain idli from unsalted batter, set aside before the rest of the batter is seasoned.
2. Cool, cut into finger-sized strips and brush with a drop of ghee.

**Notes:** It should squash easily between your thumb and finger.

### Banana and ragi pancake fingers

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** snack
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 15 min
- **Texture:** soft finger food
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg

**Ingredients**

- ½ banana, mashed
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp ragi flour
- ¼ tsp ghee

**Steps**

1. Mix mashed banana, egg and ragi flour into a thick batter.
2. Cook small, thin pancakes in ghee on low heat, 2 minutes a side, until fully cooked in the middle.
3. Cool and cut into strips.

## North Indian

### Palak dal khichdi

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** lunch
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
- **Time:** 25 min
- **Texture:** mashed

**Ingredients**

- 2 tbsp rice
- 1 tbsp moong dal
- ¼ cup spinach, chopped
- 1 pinch jeera
- 1 pinch turmeric

- 1½ cups water

**Steps**

1. Wash rice and dal and pressure cook with spinach, jeera, turmeric and water for 4 whistles.
2. Mash well or blend to the texture your baby likes, then stir in ghee. No salt.

### Soft paneer and peas mash

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
- **Time:** 10 min
- **Texture:** mashed
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 2 tbsp paneer, fresh and crumbled
- 2 tbsp green peas
- ¼ tsp ghee
- 1 pinch jeera

**Steps**

1. Boil the peas until very soft and blend them smooth.
2. Warm ghee with jeera, add the pea puree and crumbled paneer and cook for a minute.
3. Mash everything together with a little warm water. No salt.

**Notes:** Use fresh homemade paneer, which is much softer than store-bought blocks.

### Rice kheer with date paste

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** snack
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
- **Time:** 25 min
- **Texture:** porridge
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy · 🥜 Nuts

**Ingredients**

- 1 tbsp rice
- ½ cup milk, cooked into the kheer
- ½ cup water
- 1 tsp date paste
- ½ tsp almond powder, finely ground
- 1 pinch cardamom powder

**Steps**

1. Cook the rice in water until very soft and mash it.
2. Add the milk and simmer 8 minutes, stirring, until creamy.
3. Stir in date paste, finely ground almond powder and cardamom. Cool to lukewarm.

**Notes:** Date paste gives sweetness with no added sugar. Make it by soaking seedless dates and blending smooth.

### Oats porridge with almond powder

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
- **Time:** 10 min
- **Texture:** porridge
- **Good for:** 🥜 Nuts

**Ingredients**

- 2 tbsp rolled oats, ground to a powder
- ¾ cup water
- ½ tsp almond powder, finely ground
- ¼ pear, steamed and mashed

**Steps**

1. Cook oat powder in water on low heat, stirring, for 5 minutes.
2. Stir in almond powder and mashed pear. Cool to lukewarm.

### Moong dal cheela soft strips

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
- **Time:** 15 min
- **Texture:** soft finger food

**Ingredients**

- 2 tbsp moong dal, soaked 4 hours
- 1 tbsp carrot, finely grated
- 1 pinch jeera
- ½ tsp ghee

**Steps**

1. Grind the soaked dal to a smooth batter with a little water.
2. Stir in carrot and jeera.
3. Cook thick, small cheelas in ghee on low heat, covered, until soft and cooked through. Cut into strips.

## Mexican

### Black bean and avocado mash

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** lunch
- **Cuisine:** Mexican
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Texture:** mashed

**Ingredients**

- 2 tbsp black beans, cooked very soft and unsalted
- ¼ avocado, ripe
- 1 pinch cumin

**Steps**

1. Peel the skins off the beans if your baby is new to them, then mash smooth.
2. Mash in the avocado and a tiny pinch of cumin. Loosen with warm water.

**Notes:** Canned beans are salty: use home-cooked beans, or rinse low-sodium canned beans very well.

### Sweet potato and pinto bean mash

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Mexican
- **Time:** 20 min
- **Texture:** mashed

**Ingredients**

- ½ sweet potato
- 2 tbsp pinto beans, cooked very soft and unsalted
- ¼ tsp olive oil
- 1 pinch cumin

**Steps**

1. Steam the sweet potato until soft, then peel.
2. Mash with the beans, olive oil and cumin, adding warm water until smooth.

### Soft scrambled egg with avocado

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Cuisine:** Mexican
- **Time:** 8 min
- **Texture:** soft finger food
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg

**Ingredients**

- 1 egg
- ¼ avocado
- ¼ tsp butter

**Steps**

1. Beat the egg and scramble it in butter on low heat until fully cooked with no runny parts.
2. Break into soft pieces and serve with avocado mashed on the side or cut into thick strips.

### Mango and papaya puree

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** snack
- **Cuisine:** Mexican
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Texture:** puree

**Ingredients**

- ¼ mango, ripe
- ¼ cup papaya, ripe

**Steps**

### Soft corn tortilla with beans and cheese

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Mexican American
- **Time:** 10 min
- **Texture:** soft finger food
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 1 small corn tortilla
- 2 tbsp pinto beans, cooked very soft and unsalted
- 1 tbsp Monterey Jack cheese, grated

**Steps**

1. Mash the beans and spread a thin layer over half the tortilla.
2. Sprinkle a little cheese, fold, and warm in a pan until the cheese melts and the tortilla is soft.
3. Cool and cut into thin strips.

**Notes:** Cheese has some sodium, so keep it to a small sprinkle. Monterey Jack and mozzarella are lower in sodium than cotija or feta.

## Mediterranean

### Red lentil and carrot soup

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Time:** 20 min
- **Texture:** puree

**Ingredients**

- 2 tbsp masoor dal
- ½ carrot, chopped
- 1 pinch cumin
- ½ tsp olive oil
- 1 cup water

**Steps**

1. Wash the lentils and simmer with carrot, cumin and water for 15 minutes until very soft.
2. Blend smooth and stir in olive oil. No salt.

### Smooth chickpea hummus (no salt)

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** snack
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Time:** 10 min
- **Texture:** puree

**Ingredients**

- 3 tbsp chickpeas, cooked very soft and unsalted
- 1 tsp tahini
- ½ tsp olive oil
- ½ tsp lemon juice

**Steps**

1. Blend the chickpeas with tahini, olive oil, lemon juice and warm water until very smooth.
2. Serve on a spoon, or spread thinly on strips of soft toast.

**Notes:** Tahini is sesame, a common allergen. Introduce it on its own first.

## East Asian

### Okayu rice porridge with pumpkin

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** lunch
- **Cuisine:** Japanese
- **Time:** 30 min
- **Texture:** porridge

**Ingredients**

- 2 tbsp rice
- 1 cup water
- 2 tbsp pumpkin, peeled and cubed

**Steps**

1. Simmer rice and pumpkin in water on low heat, covered, for 25 minutes until very soft.
2. Mash or blend smooth.

### Egg congee with carrot

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Chinese
- **Time:** 30 min
- **Texture:** porridge
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg

**Ingredients**

- 2 tbsp rice
- 1¼ cups water
- 2 tbsp carrot, grated
- 1 egg, beaten

**Steps**

1. Simmer rice, carrot and water for 25 minutes until it breaks down into a porridge.
2. Stream in the beaten egg while stirring and cook 2 more minutes until the egg is fully cooked.
3. Cool to lukewarm. No soy sauce for the baby.

### Soft tofu and sweet potato mash

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** lunch
- **Serves:** 2
- **Cuisine:** Japanese
- **Time:** 15 min
- **Texture:** mashed

**Ingredients**

- 2 tbsp silken tofu
- ¼ sweet potato

**Steps**

1. Steam the sweet potato until soft and peel.
2. Warm the tofu for a minute in the steamer, then mash everything together until smooth.
