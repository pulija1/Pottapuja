# Leftover makeovers

Yesterday's curry turned into something new: roti quesadillas, kathi rolls, kari dosa and tacos. When a family dinner on the calendar is a curry (chicken, paneer, dal, rajma or chole, a kura or sambar), the next day's suggestions offer one of these, and the Recipes tab lists them under **Leftover makeovers**.

A recipe's **Leftover** line says what it takes: `chicken curry`, `paneer curry`, `beans curry` (rajma, chole, black beans), `vegetable curry` (kura, sabzi, poriyal), `dal`, `sambar`, or `any curry`. Ingredients that start with "leftover" never go on the shopping list.

Food safety for leftovers: put the curry in the fridge within 2 hours of cooking, use it the next day, and reheat it until it is steaming hot all the way through. Don't reheat it a second time. Leftovers are for the family and the 4-year-old; give the baby freshly made food.

Curries worth cooking double: half for dinner, half for tomorrow's makeover.

### Hyderabadi chicken curry (mild)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Telugu (Hyderabadi)
- **Time:** 45 min
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 750 g chicken, curry cut
- 3 onion, thinly sliced
- 2 tomato, chopped
- 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
- ½ cup curd
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 2 tbsp mint leaves
- 2 tbsp coriander leaves
- 3 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt

**Steps**

1. Marinate the chicken in curd, turmeric and ginger garlic paste for 30 minutes.
2. Fry the onions in oil until deep golden, add the tomatoes and cook until soft.
3. Add the chicken and cook covered 20 minutes, stirring now and then, until fully cooked.
4. Baby portion: take out a small piece of plain cooked chicken before salt and spice and shred it very finely into mashed rice.
5. Kid portion: take it out now, then add chilli powder, coriander powder, garam masala and salt to the rest and simmer 5 minutes. Finish with mint and coriander leaves.

**Notes:** Makes enough for two meals. Shred tomorrow's half off the bone before it goes in the fridge.

### Chettinad-style chicken kuzhambu (mild)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Tamil (Chettinad)
- **Time:** 45 min

**Ingredients**

- 750 g chicken, curry cut
- 2 onion, finely chopped
- 2 tomato, chopped
- 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
- ½ cup grated coconut
- 1 tsp fennel seeds
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 8 curry leaves
- 3 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp salt

**Steps**

1. Dry roast the coconut, fennel and pepper until fragrant, cool, and grind to a paste with a little water.
2. Fry the onion and curry leaves in oil until golden, add ginger garlic paste and tomatoes and cook until soft.
3. Add the chicken and turmeric and cook covered 20 minutes until fully cooked.
4. Baby portion: take out a small piece of plain cooked chicken before salt and spice and shred it very finely into mashed rice.
5. Kid portion: take it out now, then add the ground paste, coriander powder, salt and 1 cup water to the rest and simmer 10 minutes.

**Notes:** Lovely with rice or idiyappam, and the leftovers make kari dosai the next day.

### Chicken tikka masala (mild)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
- **Time:** 50 min
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 750 g boneless chicken, cut in pieces
- ½ cup curd
- 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
- 1 tsp kashmiri chilli powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 2 onion, finely chopped
- 3 tomato, pureed
- ¼ cup cream
- 1 tsp kasuri methi
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tsp salt

**Steps**

1. Marinate the chicken in curd and ginger garlic paste for 30 minutes, then sear it in a hot pan until browned and fully cooked.
2. Baby portion: take out a small piece of plain chicken and shred it very finely into mashed rice.
3. Melt the butter, cook the onion until soft, add the tomato puree and cook 10 minutes until thick.
4. Kid portion: take out a little sauce and chicken now. Add the chilli powder, garam masala, kasuri methi and salt to the rest, stir in the cream and simmer the chicken in it 5 minutes.

**Notes:** Boneless chicken makes the next day's rolls and quesadillas quick.

## Next-day makeovers for the 4-year-old

### Roti chicken curry quesadilla

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** lunchbox
- **Cuisine:** Telugu / Mexican fusion
- **Time:** 10 min
- **Leftover:** chicken curry
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy
- **Family favorite:** yes

**Ingredients**

- 2 roti
- ½ cup leftover chicken curry, boneless and shredded
- ¼ cup cheese, grated
- 1 tbsp coriander leaves, chopped
- 1 tsp butter

**Steps**

1. Heat the leftover curry until steaming hot, take the chicken out of the gravy and shred it, keeping it fairly dry.
2. Lay one roti in a pan, spread half the cheese, the chicken and coriander, top with the rest of the cheese and the second roti.
3. Toast in butter 2 minutes a side until the cheese melts, then cut into wedges.

**Notes:** Pick out any bones before shredding. Pack it in an insulated lunchbox with a spoon of curd to dip.

### Roti paneer quesadilla

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** lunchbox
- **Cuisine:** North Indian / Mexican fusion
- **Time:** 10 min
- **Leftover:** paneer curry
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 2 roti
- ½ cup leftover paneer curry, mashed
- 2 tbsp cheese, grated
- 1 tsp butter

**Steps**

1. Heat the leftover paneer curry until steaming hot and mash it with a fork so it spreads.
2. Spread it on one roti, sprinkle the cheese and cover with the second roti.
3. Toast in butter 2 minutes a side and cut into wedges.

### Dal paratha (pappu paratha)

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** lunchbox
- **Cuisine:** North Indian / Telugu
- **Time:** 20 min
- **Leftover:** dal
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 1 cup whole wheat flour (atta)
- ½ cup leftover dal
- 1 tbsp coriander leaves, chopped
- 1 tsp ghee
- ¼ cup curd, to serve

**Steps**

1. Knead the flour with the leftover dal and coriander leaves into a soft dough, adding water only if needed. Rest 10 minutes.
2. Roll into small parathas and cook on a hot tawa with a little ghee until golden spots show on both sides.
3. Pack with curd.

**Notes:** Works with tomato pappu, palakura pappu or dal tadka. Thick dal needs less water.

### Sambar idli

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Cuisine:** Tamil
- **Time:** 10 min
- **Leftover:** sambar

**Ingredients**

- 4 idli
- 1 cup leftover sambar
- 1 tsp ghee

**Steps**

1. Boil the leftover sambar until steaming, adding a splash of water if it has thickened.
2. Cut the idlis into quarters and soak them in the hot sambar for 2 minutes.
3. Drizzle with ghee and serve warm.

**Notes:** Add a boiled egg on the side for the day's egg.

### Kura roti roll

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** lunchbox
- **Cuisine:** Telugu
- **Time:** 10 min
- **Leftover:** vegetable curry
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 2 roti
- ½ cup leftover vegetable curry
- 2 tbsp cheese, grated

**Steps**

1. Heat the leftover kura in a pan until steaming hot and dry, mashing big pieces.
2. Spread it along the middle of each roti, sprinkle cheese and roll up tightly.
3. Toast the rolls seam side down for a minute so they stay closed, then cut in half.

**Notes:** Good with bangaladumpa (potato), cabbage, beans or bendakaya fry.

### Rajma or chole burrito with roti

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** lunchbox
- **Cuisine:** North Indian / Mexican fusion
- **Time:** 10 min
- **Leftover:** beans curry
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 2 roti
- ½ cup leftover beans curry
- ¼ cup cooked rice
- 2 tbsp cheese, grated
- 2 tbsp cucumber, finely chopped

**Steps**

1. Heat the leftover rajma or chole until steaming and dry, mashing the beans lightly so they don't roll out.
2. Spread rice and beans along each roti, top with cheese and cucumber and roll up like a burrito.
3. Toast the rolls in a dry pan and cut in half.

**Notes:** Use rice cooked fresh that morning, not leftover rice.

## Next-day makeovers for the family

### Chicken curry kathi roll with egg

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
- **Time:** 20 min
- **Leftover:** chicken curry, paneer curry
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg

**Ingredients**

- 4 roti
- 2 cups leftover chicken curry, boneless and shredded
- 4 egg
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp green chutney
- 1 tbsp oil

**Steps**

1. Heat the leftover curry in a pan, stirring, until steaming hot and thick enough to hold in a roll.
2. Beat one egg per roti. Pour an egg into a hot oiled pan, lay a roti on top, and flip after a minute so the egg cooks onto the roti.
3. Spread chutney, add the curry and onion with a squeeze of lemon, and roll up in paper.
4. Kid portion: skip the chutney and raw onion, and add a little grated cheese instead.

**Notes:** Use leftover paneer curry the same way. Give the baby freshly made food, not leftovers.

### Kari dosai (Madurai-style chicken curry dosa)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Tamil
- **Time:** 20 min
- **Leftover:** chicken curry
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg

**Ingredients**

- 2 cups dosa batter
- 1½ cups leftover chicken curry, boneless and shredded
- 4 egg
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp coriander leaves, chopped
- 2 tbsp oil

**Steps**

1. Heat the leftover curry until steaming hot and cook off most of the gravy.
2. Spread a thick dosa on a hot tawa, break an egg on top and spread it, then scatter chicken, onion and coriander.
3. Drizzle oil around, cover and cook on medium until the egg is fully set, then flip for a minute.
4. Kid portion: make a small one without onion, and cook the egg right through.

### Roti chicken curry tacos with cabbage slaw

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Mexican / Telugu fusion
- **Time:** 15 min
- **Leftover:** chicken curry
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 6 roti
- 2 cups leftover chicken curry, boneless and shredded
- 2 cups cabbage, finely shredded
- 1 carrot, grated
- 1 lime
- ½ cup curd
- ½ cup cheese, grated
- 2 tbsp coriander leaves, chopped

**Steps**

1. Toss the cabbage and carrot with lime juice and a pinch of salt for the slaw.
2. Heat the leftover curry until steaming hot and cook off most of the gravy.
3. Warm the roti, cut them in half or use small ones, and fill with chicken, slaw, cheese, curd and coriander.

**Notes:** For the 4-year-old, grate the carrot finely and skip the lime on their slaw if it's too sour.

### Leftover curry roti enchilada bake

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Mexican fusion
- **Time:** 30 min
- **Leftover:** any curry
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 6 roti
- 3 cups leftover curry
- 1 cup tomato puree
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 cup cheese, grated
- ½ cup sweet corn
- 2 tbsp coriander leaves, chopped

**Steps**

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Simmer the tomato puree with cumin for 5 minutes for the sauce.
2. Heat the leftover curry until steaming and mix in the corn.
3. Roll the curry in the roti, lay them in a baking dish, pour the sauce over and cover with cheese.
4. Bake 15 minutes until bubbling and golden. Scatter coriander on top.

**Notes:** Works with chicken, paneer, rajma, chole or a vegetable kura.
