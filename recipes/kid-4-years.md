# Recipes for the 4-year-old

School-age recipes from the kitchens we cook from every week: South Indian, North Indian, Indian American, Mexican American, and a few from Japan, Korea, Italy and the Middle East. Each one is sized for one child.

The daily goal for the 4-year-old is **one egg, one milk or dairy food, and some nuts**. Every recipe below says what it gives under **Good for**, so you can scan for 🥚, 🥛 and 🥜. The [weekly rotation in the README](README.md#a-week-for-the-4-year-old) shows one easy way to hit all three every day.

Nuts for a 4-year-old are always **chopped, slivered, powdered or a nut butter**, never whole. Recipes with nuts that go to school list a **Nut-free swap** in case the school or a class turns nut-free.

## Breakfast: an egg every day

### Masala egg bhurji with buttered toast fingers

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Cuisine:** Indian American
- **Time:** 10 min
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg · 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 2 eggs
- 1 tbsp milk
- 2 tbsp cheddar cheese, grated
- ¼ onion, very finely chopped
- ¼ tomato, seeds removed and chopped
- 1 pinch turmeric
- 1 tbsp coriander leaves, chopped
- 1 tsp butter
- 1 slice whole wheat bread
- 1 pinch salt

**Steps**

1. Whisk the eggs with milk, turmeric and salt.
2. Melt butter in a pan and soften the onion for 2 minutes, then add tomato and cook until mushy.
3. Pour in the eggs and stir slowly on low heat until fully set but still soft. Fold in cheese and coriander.
4. Toast the bread, butter it and cut it into fingers for scooping.

**Notes:** No green chilli for the kid's portion. For the lunchbox, stuff the bhurji into a roti and roll it up.

### Huevos rancheros soft tacos (mild)

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Cuisine:** Mexican American
- **Time:** 15 min
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg · 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 1 egg
- 2 small corn tortillas
- ¼ cup black beans, cooked and mashed
- 2 tbsp mild salsa
- 2 tbsp queso fresco, crumbled
- ¼ avocado, sliced
- 1 tsp oil
- 1 pinch salt

**Steps**

1. Warm the mashed black beans with a spoon of water and a pinch of salt.
2. Fry the egg in oil until the white and yolk are fully set, then cut it into strips.
3. Warm the tortillas on a dry pan until soft.
4. Spread beans on each tortilla, add egg strips, salsa, queso fresco and avocado. Fold in half.

**Notes:** Pick a "mild" salsa or make one with tomato, onion and coriander only.

### Egg, potato and cheese breakfast burrito

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Cuisine:** Mexican American
- **Time:** 20 min
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg · 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 1 egg
- 1 small potato, diced small
- 1 flour tortilla
- 2 tbsp Monterey Jack cheese, grated
- 1 tbsp bell pepper, finely chopped
- 1 pinch cumin
- 1 tsp oil
- 1 pinch salt

**Steps**

1. Pan-fry the diced potato in oil with cumin and salt, covered, for 8 minutes until soft and golden.
2. Add bell pepper for 1 minute, then pour in the beaten egg and scramble until fully cooked.
3. Warm the tortilla, sprinkle cheese down the middle, add the filling and roll up tightly, tucking in the sides.
4. Toast seam side down for a minute so it stays closed. Cut in half.

**Notes:** Makes a good freezer breakfast: wrap in foil, freeze, and reheat for 1 minute.

### French toast sticks with cardamom and almond powder

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Cuisine:** Indian American
- **Time:** 15 min
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg · 🥛 Milk/dairy · 🥜 Nuts
- **Nut-free swap:** Leave out the almond powder and add a pinch more cinnamon.
- **Air fryer:** 8 min at 180°C. Dip the bread sticks, lay them on a greased tray or parchment in one layer and turn at 4 minutes, until the egg is fully set and golden.

**Ingredients**

- 1 egg
- ¼ cup milk
- 1 tbsp almond powder
- 1 pinch cardamom powder
- 1 pinch cinnamon
- 2 slices whole wheat bread, cut into sticks
- 1 tsp butter
- ½ banana, sliced

**Steps**

1. Whisk egg, milk, almond powder, cardamom and cinnamon in a flat dish.
2. Dip the bread sticks for a few seconds on each side.
3. Cook in butter on medium heat until golden and the egg is fully cooked, about 2 minutes per side.
4. Serve with banana slices.

**Notes:** Three goals in one plate. A drizzle of honey is fine at 4 years.

### Kerala egg roast with soft appam (mild)

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Cuisine:** Kerala
- **Time:** 25 min
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg

**Ingredients**

- 1 egg, hard boiled and peeled
- ½ onion, thinly sliced
- ½ tomato, chopped
- ¼ tsp ginger, grated
- ¼ tsp coriander powder
- 1 pinch turmeric
- 2 tbsp coconut milk
- 1 tsp coconut oil
- 6 curry leaves
- 1 pinch salt
- 2 appam, or soft dosa

**Steps**

1. Slow-cook the onion in coconut oil with curry leaves for 8 to 10 minutes until soft and golden.
2. Add ginger, tomato, turmeric, coriander powder and salt, and cook until jammy.
3. Stir in coconut milk, then the egg with a few shallow cuts so it takes in the masala. Simmer 2 minutes.
4. Serve with appam, cutting the egg into quarters for the kid.

**Notes:** The grown-up version gets chilli powder and pepper; take the kid's portion out first.

### Tamagoyaki rolled omelette

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Cuisine:** Japanese
- **Time:** 10 min
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg · 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 2 eggs
- 1 tbsp milk
- ½ tsp sugar
- ½ tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp oil
- ½ cup rice, cooked

**Steps**

1. Whisk eggs with milk, sugar and soy sauce.
2. Oil a small pan, pour in a thin layer, and when almost set roll it to one side.
3. Pour in another thin layer under the roll, let it set and roll again. Repeat until the egg is used.
4. Rest for a minute, then slice into rounds. Serve with rice.

**Notes:** Packs well cold in a bento lunchbox.

### Egg rava upma

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 20 min
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg

**Ingredients**

- ⅓ cup rava
- 1 egg
- ¼ onion, chopped
- 2 tbsp carrot, grated
- ½ tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp urad dal
- 5 curry leaves
- 1 tsp ghee
- 1 cup water
- 1 pinch salt

**Steps**

1. Dry roast rava until fragrant and set aside.
2. Temper mustard seeds, urad dal and curry leaves in ghee, then soften onion and carrot.
3. Push the vegetables aside, scramble the egg in the pan until fully cooked, and mix it in.
4. Add water and salt, bring to a boil, stream in rava while stirring, cover and cook 3 minutes.

### Boiled egg podi bites

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Cuisine:** Andhra
- **Time:** 15 min
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg
- **Air fryer:** 15 min at 135°C for the eggs, straight into the basket, then into cold water before peeling. Fully cooked, no pan to watch.

**Ingredients**

- 1 egg
- ½ tsp idli podi, mild
- ½ tsp ghee
- 3 mini idlis, or one idli cut into four

**Steps**

1. Hard boil the egg for 10 minutes, cool in cold water and peel.
2. Cut into quarters, drizzle with warm ghee and sprinkle with mild podi.
3. Serve with the idlis tossed in the leftover ghee and podi.

**Notes:** The fastest egg of the day: boil a few eggs on Sunday and keep them in the fridge for up to 5 days.

### Spinach and cheddar egg muffins

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** lunchbox
- **Serves:** 3 (2 mini muffins each)
- **Cuisine:** American
- **Time:** 25 min
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg · 🥛 Milk/dairy
- **Air fryer:** 12 min at 160°C in silicone muffin cups, until fully set in the centre.
- **Oven:** 18 min at 175°C (350°F) in a mini muffin tin, as in the steps.

**Ingredients**

- 3 eggs
- 2 tbsp milk
- ¼ cup spinach, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp bell pepper, finely chopped
- ¼ cup cheddar cheese, grated
- 1 pinch salt
- 1 tsp oil

**Steps**

1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and oil 6 cups of a mini muffin tin.
2. Whisk eggs, milk and salt. Stir in spinach, bell pepper and cheese.
3. Pour into the cups and bake 15 to 18 minutes, until puffed and fully set in the centre.
4. Cool on a rack before packing.

**Notes:** Makes 6 mini muffins. Keeps 3 days in the fridge; pack 2 or 3 per lunchbox.

### Rajma rice with cucumber raita

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** lunchbox
- **Cuisine:** Punjabi
- **Time:** 40 min
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- ¼ cup rajma, soaked overnight
- ½ tomato, pureed
- ¼ onion, finely chopped
- ¼ tsp ginger garlic paste
- ¼ tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp ghee
- ¾ cup rice, cooked
- ¼ cup curd
- 2 tbsp cucumber, grated
- 1 pinch salt

**Steps**

1. Pressure cook the soaked rajma for 6 to 7 whistles until very soft.

**Notes:** Cook a big pot of rajma for family dinner and keep a kid portion aside before adding chilli.

### Ragi cookies (jaggery, no egg)

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** snack
- **Serves:** 8 (2 small cookies each)
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 30 min
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy
- **Snack box:** tiny
- **Prep ahead:** weekend · 30 min · keeps 7 days in an airtight tin
- **Oven:** 15 min at 170°C (340°F), as in the steps.
- **Air fryer:** 8 min at 160°C, in batches on parchment.

**Ingredients**

- ½ cup ragi flour
- ½ cup whole wheat flour
- ¼ cup butter, soft
- ¼ cup jaggery, powdered
- 1 pinch cardamom powder
- 2 tbsp milk

**Steps**

**Notes:** Nut-free and egg-free. Bake a batch at the weekend for the whole school week. Ragi is fine for the 4-year-old (the baby's Never use list is separate).

- 2 tbsp rice flour
- 2 tbsp curd
- ¼ onion
- 3 tbsp coconut
- ¼ tsp salt

**Steps**

1. Mix ragi flour, rice flour, curd, salt and water into a thin batter.
2. Pour onto a hot tawa, sprinkle chopped onion and cook till crisp.
3. Grind coconut with a little water and salt for a mild chutney.

### Ven pongal

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Serves:** 2
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 25 min

**Ingredients**

- ½ cup rice
- ¼ cup moong dal
- 1 tbsp ghee
- ½ tsp jeera
- ½ tsp ginger
- ½ tsp salt

**Steps**

1. Pressure cook rice and moong dal soft.
2. Temper jeera and ginger in ghee, pour over and mix with salt.
3. Use a pinch of crushed pepper only if your child likes it.
