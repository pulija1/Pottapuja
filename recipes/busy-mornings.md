# Busy weekday mornings

Breakfasts for school days, when everyone leaves the house early. The family likes a **warm breakfast**: most of these are **made the night before and warmed in the microwave** in the morning (egg burritos, upma, parathas, idlis). On the busiest days there is **no-cook** food made that morning (bread with peanut butter or avocado). In the app, weekday breakfast suggestions come from here first, warm ones first, then no-cook. The baby's breakfasts here are fruit, so the baby gets a fruit every morning.

- The **Busy morning** line says which kind it is: `make ahead` or `no cook`.
- The **Reheat** line says how to warm a make-ahead breakfast. A make-ahead breakfast without it is eaten cold.
- The **Side dip** line names an easy dip from the [Side dips](#side-dips) section to serve with it, like a yogurt dip with an egg burrito. Dips keep 2 to 4 days in the fridge, so make one on Sunday.

Peanut butter is fine at home. These are breakfasts, not lunchbox food, so the school's nut-free rule doesn't apply; the app still keeps nuts out of the lunchbox and snack box when the school is nut-free.

## Family: made the night before, warmed in the morning

### Egg and potato breakfast burritos (made the night before)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Busy morning:** make ahead
- **Prep ahead:** weekend · 35 min · make 8, keeps 3 days in the fridge or a month in the freezer
- **Reheat:** 1 to 1½ min in the microwave, wrapped in a damp paper towel, turning once
- **Side dip:** Mint yogurt dip, Mild salsa
- **Cuisine:** Mexican American
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Serves:** 4 (1 burrito each)
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg · 🥛 Milk/dairy
- **Air fryer:** 5 min at 180°C. Reheat from the fridge until crisp outside.

**Ingredients**

- 4 flour tortilla
- 5 egg
- 2 potato, small dice
- ½ cup black beans, drained
- ½ cup cheddar cheese, grated
- ¼ onion, finely chopped
- ½ tsp cumin
- 1 tbsp oil
- Salt, a pinch

**Steps**

1. At night: fry the potato and onion in oil with the cumin and salt until the potato is soft, about 10 minutes.
2. Add the beans, then the beaten eggs, and scramble until fully cooked with no runny egg.
3. Cool for 10 minutes, spoon onto the tortillas, top with cheese, and roll up tightly.
4. Wrap each burrito in foil or cling film and keep in the fridge (3 days) or the freezer (1 month).
5. In the morning: unwrap, wrap in a damp paper towel and microwave 1 to 1½ minutes, turning once. From frozen, 2½ minutes.
6. Serve with the mint yogurt dip or salsa. Cut the 4-year-old's in half and check it isn't too hot inside.

**Notes:** Make eight on Sunday and freeze half, so the week is covered.

### Vegetable upma (made the night before)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Busy morning:** make ahead
- **Prep ahead:** night before · 15 min · keeps a day in the fridge
- **Reheat:** 1½ to 2 min in the microwave with 2 tbsp water, covered, stir halfway
- **Side dip:** Coconut chutney
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Serves:** 3

**Ingredients**

- 1 cup rava
- 1 carrot, finely chopped
- ½ cup peas
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp urad dal
- 8 curry leaves
- 2 tbsp ghee
- 2½ cups water
- Salt, to taste

**Steps**

1. At night: dry-roast the rava until it smells toasty and set it aside.
2. Heat ghee, splutter the mustard seeds, urad dal and curry leaves, then soften the onion, carrot and peas.
3. Add the water and salt, bring to a boil, and stir in the rava slowly so there are no lumps. Cook covered on low for 3 minutes.
4. Cool and keep in a closed box in the fridge.
5. In the morning: sprinkle with 2 tablespoons of water, cover and microwave 1½ to 2 minutes, stirring halfway. It turns soft again.

### Aloo paratha (made the night before)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Busy morning:** make ahead
- **Prep ahead:** weekend · 40 min · keeps 3 days in the fridge or a month in the freezer
- **Reheat:** 30 to 40 sec in the microwave under a damp paper towel, or 1 min on a hot tawa
- **Side dip:** Mint yogurt dip, Cucumber raita dip
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Serves:** 4 (2 parathas each)
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 2 cups atta
- 3 potato, boiled
- ½ tsp jeera
- ½ tsp garam masala
- 2 tbsp coriander leaves, chopped
- 2 tbsp ghee
- Salt, to taste

**Steps**

1. At night: knead the atta with water and a pinch of salt into a soft dough.
2. Mash the boiled potatoes with jeera, garam masala, coriander and salt.
3. Stuff balls of dough with the potato, roll out, and cook on a hot tawa with ghee until golden on both sides.
4. Cool, stack with paper between them, and keep in a closed box in the fridge.
5. In the morning: warm two at a time in the microwave under a damp paper towel, or on the tawa if there's a minute more.

**Notes:** Keep the 4-year-old's portion without chilli. Parathas travel well for eating on the way.

### Baked banana oatmeal (made the night before)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Busy morning:** make ahead
- **Prep ahead:** weekend · 40 min · bakes 30 min, keeps 4 days in the fridge
- **Reheat:** 1 min in the microwave
- **Side dip:** Cinnamon yogurt dip
- **Cuisine:** American
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Serves:** 6 (1 square each)
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg · 🥛 Milk/dairy
- **Oven:** 30 min at 180°C. Bake at night until set and golden.

**Ingredients**

- 2 cups rolled oats
- 3 banana, mashed
- 2 egg
- 1½ cups milk
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 2 tbsp butter, melted
- ¼ cup raisins

**Steps**

1. At night: mix everything in a greased baking dish.
2. Bake at 180°C for 30 minutes until set in the middle. Cool, cut into six squares, and keep in the fridge.
3. In the morning: warm a square for 1 minute in the microwave and serve with the cinnamon yogurt dip.

**Notes:** Chop the raisins for the 4-year-old. Keeps 4 days.

### Overnight oats with banana and cinnamon

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Busy morning:** make ahead
- **Prep ahead:** night before · 5 min
- **Reheat:** 1½ min in the microwave, stir halfway
- **Cuisine:** American
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Serves:** 3
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 1½ cups rolled oats
- 1½ cups milk
- ½ cup curd
- 2 banana
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- 2 tbsp raisins

**Steps**

1. At night: stir the oats, milk, curd and cinnamon together in a box or three jars.
2. Mash one banana into the oats, cover, and put it in the fridge overnight.
3. In the morning: warm for 1½ minutes in the microwave, stirring halfway, with a splash of milk if thick. Slice the other banana on top and add the raisins for the grown-ups.
4. For the 4-year-old, chop the raisins or leave them out.

**Notes:** Keeps 2 days in the fridge, so make a double batch on Sunday night. Add a spoon of peanut butter at home if you like.

- **For:** family

- **Busy morning:** make ahead
- **Prep ahead:** night before · 20 min · with batter that's ready
- **Reheat:** 1 to 2 min in the microwave, sprinkled with water and covered
- **Side dip:** Coconut chutney, Tomato chutney
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Serves:** 3

**Ingredients**

- 3 cups idli/dosa batter
- 2 tbsp idli podi
- 2 tbsp ghee

**Steps**

1. At night: steam the idlis for 12 minutes, cool, and keep them in a closed box in the fridge.

**Notes:** Pack two idlis in a box to eat in the car. Grind the batter on the weekend; it keeps 4 to 5 days in the fridge.

### Curd rice with pomegranate (cooked the night before)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Busy morning:** make ahead
- **Prep ahead:** night before · 10 min · with rice from dinner
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Serves:** 3
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 1 cup rice
- 2 cups curd
- ½ cup milk
- ½ cup pomegranate
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 8 curry leaves
- 1 tsp ghee
- Salt, to taste

**Steps**

1. At night: cook the rice soft, mash it a little, and let it cool.
2. Stir in the curd, milk and salt. The milk stops it turning sour overnight. Fry the mustard seeds and curry leaves in ghee and pour them over.
3. Cover and keep in the fridge.
4. In the morning: stir, loosen with a little milk if thick, and top with pomegranate.

**Notes:** Keep the pomegranate off the baby's portion; the seeds are a choking risk under 12 months.

## Side dips

Easy dips to serve on the side of a breakfast. Each one keeps 2 to 4 days in a closed box in the fridge.

### Mint yogurt dip

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** side
- **Busy morning:** make ahead
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Serves:** 2
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 1 cup curd, thick
