# Busy weekday mornings

Breakfasts for school days, when everyone leaves the house early. Each one is either **made the night before** (overnight oats, idlis steamed in the evening, eggs boiled ahead) or **no-cook** (bread with peanut butter or avocado, fruit with curd). In the app, weekday breakfast suggestions come from here first. The baby's breakfasts here are fruit, so the baby gets a fruit every morning.

The **Busy morning** line says which kind it is: `make ahead` or `no cook`.

Peanut butter is fine at home. These are breakfasts, not lunchbox food, so the school's nut-free rule doesn't apply; the app still keeps nuts out of the lunchbox and snack box when the school is nut-free.

## Family: made the night before

### Overnight oats with banana and cinnamon

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Busy morning:** make ahead
- **Cuisine:** American
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Serves:** 3
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 1½ cups rolled oats
- 1½ cups milk
- ½ cup curd
- 2 banana
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- 2 tbsp raisins

**Steps**

1. At night: stir the oats, milk, curd and cinnamon together in a box or three jars.
2. Mash one banana into the oats, cover, and put it in the fridge overnight.
3. In the morning: slice the other banana on top, add the raisins for the grown-ups, and eat cold or warm for 1 minute in the microwave.
4. For the 4-year-old, chop the raisins or leave them out.

**Notes:** Keeps 2 days in the fridge, so make a double batch on Sunday night. Add a spoon of peanut butter at home if you like.

### Idli with podi and ghee (steamed the night before)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Busy morning:** make ahead
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Serves:** 3

**Ingredients**

- 3 cups idli/dosa batter
- 2 tbsp idli podi
- 2 tbsp ghee

**Steps**

1. At night: steam the idlis for 12 minutes, cool, and keep them in a closed box in the fridge.
2. In the morning: sprinkle the idlis with a little water and warm them for 1 to 2 minutes in the microwave or 5 minutes in the steamer.
3. Serve with podi mixed into ghee. Give the 4-year-old idli with ghee and a little podi on the side.

**Notes:** Pack two idlis in a box to eat in the car. Grind the batter on the weekend; it keeps 4 to 5 days in the fridge.

### Boiled egg and cheese sandwich

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Busy morning:** make ahead
- **Cuisine:** American
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Serves:** 3
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg · 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 4 egg
- 6 slices whole wheat bread
- 3 slices cheddar cheese
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 pinch black pepper

**Steps**

1. At night: boil the eggs for 10 minutes until the yolk is fully set, cool them in cold water, and keep them in the fridge in their shells.
2. In the morning: butter the bread, slice the eggs, and lay them on with the cheese and a pinch of black pepper.
3. Close the sandwiches and cut the 4-year-old's into fingers.

**Notes:** Boil a dozen eggs on Sunday night for the week; peeled or not, they keep 5 days in the fridge.

### Curd rice with pomegranate (cooked the night before)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Busy morning:** make ahead
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Serves:** 3
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 1 cup rice
- 2 cups curd
- ½ cup milk
- ½ cup pomegranate
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 8 curry leaves
- 1 tsp ghee
- Salt, to taste

**Steps**

1. At night: cook the rice soft, mash it a little, and let it cool.
2. Stir in the curd, milk and salt. The milk stops it turning sour overnight. Fry the mustard seeds and curry leaves in ghee and pour them over.
3. Cover and keep in the fridge.
4. In the morning: stir, loosen with a little milk if thick, and top with pomegranate.

**Notes:** Keep the pomegranate off the baby's portion; the seeds are a choking risk under 12 months.

### Thepla with curd (made the night before)

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Busy morning:** make ahead
- **Cuisine:** North Indian
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Serves:** 3
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 2 cups atta
- 1 cup methi leaves, chopped
- ½ cup curd
- ½ tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp jeera
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 cup curd, to serve
- Salt, to taste

**Steps**

1. At night: mix the atta, methi, ½ cup curd, turmeric, jeera, salt and oil into a soft dough with a little water.
2. Roll thin and cook on a hot tawa with a little oil until spotted on both sides.
3. Cool, stack, and keep wrapped in a cloth in a closed box.
4. In the morning: eat at room temperature or warm on the tawa for 20 seconds each, with curd.

**Notes:** Theplas keep 2 to 3 days and travel well, so they are good for eating on the way.

### Chia pudding with mango

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Busy morning:** make ahead
- **Cuisine:** American
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Serves:** 3
- **Good for:** 🥛 Milk/dairy

**Ingredients**

- 6 tbsp chia seeds
- 2 cups milk
- 1 mango, chopped
- ½ tsp cardamom powder

**Steps**

1. At night: stir the chia seeds and cardamom into the milk, wait 5 minutes, and stir again so there are no lumps.
2. Cover and keep in the fridge overnight.
3. In the morning: stir and top with chopped mango.

**Notes:** For the 4-year-old, stir well so the chia is fully soaked. Not for the baby.

## Family: no-cook

### Peanut butter banana toast

- **For:** family
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Busy morning:** no cook
- **Cuisine:** American
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Serves:** 3
- **Good for:** 🥜 Nuts
- **Nut-free swap:** use sunflower seed butter

**Ingredients**

- 6 slices whole wheat bread
- 4 tbsp peanut butter
- 2 banana
- ½ tsp cinnamon

**Steps**

1. Toast the bread, or leave it soft.
2. Spread a thin layer of peanut butter and top with sliced banana and a pinch of cinnamon.
3. Cut the 4-year-old's into fingers. Spread the peanut butter thin; thick spoonfuls are hard for a small child to swallow.

**Notes:** At home only when the school is nut-free: wash hands and wipe faces before school.

4. Keep a spoon of plain mashed avocado aside for the baby before the salt goes in.

## For the 4-year-old

### Make-ahead egg muffins with spinach and cheese

- **For:** kid
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Busy morning:** make ahead
- **Cuisine:** American
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Serves:** 6 (1 muffin each)
- **Good for:** 🥚 Egg · 🥛 Milk/dairy
- **Oven:** 20 min at 180°C. Bake at night in a greased muffin tray until set in the middle.
- **Air fryer:** 12 min at 160°C. In silicone cups, at night.

**Ingredients**

- 6 egg
- ¼ cup milk
- ½ cup cheddar cheese, grated
- ½ cup spinach, finely chopped
- ¼ bell pepper, finely chopped
- Salt, a pinch

**Steps**

1. At night: whisk the eggs with the milk and salt, and stir in the spinach, pepper and cheese.
2. Pour into a greased muffin tray and bake until fully set in the middle, about 20 minutes.
3. Cool and keep in a closed box in the fridge.
4. In the morning: warm one or two for 30 seconds in the microwave.

**Notes:** Keeps 4 days in the fridge, so one batch covers most of the school week.

## For the baby: a fruit every morning

### Mashed banana and avocado

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Busy morning:** no cook
- **Cuisine:** American
- **Time:** 3 min
- **Texture:** mashed

**Ingredients**

- ½ banana
- ¼ avocado

**Steps**

1. Mash a ripe banana and a ripe avocado together with a fork until smooth.
2. Loosen with a little breast milk or formula if it's thick.

**Notes:** Two fruits, no cooking. Brown spots on the banana mean it's sweet and soft enough.

### Steamed apple and pear puree

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Busy morning:** make ahead
- **Cuisine:** American
- **Time:** 5 min
- **Serves:** 4
- **Texture:** puree

**Ingredients**

- 1 apple
- 1 pear

**Steps**

1. At night: peel, core and chop the apple and pear, steam for 10 minutes until very soft, and blend smooth.
2. Keep in a closed box in the fridge (3 days) or freeze in an ice-cube tray.
3. In the morning: take out a portion and warm it to lukewarm, or serve at room temperature.

### Ragi porridge with banana (soaked the night before)

- **For:** baby
- **Meal:** breakfast
- **Busy morning:** make ahead
- **Cuisine:** South Indian
- **Time:** 6 min
- **Texture:** porridge

**Ingredients**

- 2 tbsp ragi flour
- 1 cup water
- ½ banana

**Steps**

1. At night: whisk the ragi flour into the cold water in the pan, cover and leave it on the counter.
2. In the morning: stir and cook on low heat for 4 to 5 minutes until thick. Soaking makes it cook faster.
3. Cool to lukewarm and stir in mashed ripe banana.
